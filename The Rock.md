
**1. CALLED: Peter Meets Jesus**

**Theme:** Jesus calls ordinary people into extraordinary purpose.

- **Andrew follows John the Baptist: Peter does not**  
    _John 1:35–40_
- **Andrew brings Peter to Jesus: Peter’s first encounter**  
    _John 1:40–42_
- **Jesus enters Peter’s home: healing his mother‑in‑law**  
    _Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31_
- **The miraculous catch: Peter’s awakening**  
    _Luke 5:1–11_
- **The formal call to discipleship: “Follow Me”**  
    _Luke 5:10–11; Matthew 4:18–20; Mark 1:16–18_

**2. CONFLICTED — Zeal Without Surrender**

**Theme:** Peter’s passion is real, but his inner life is unstable.

- **Peter walks on water: faith and fear collide**  
    _Matthew 14:22–33_
- **Peter’s bold vow at the Last Supper**  
    _Luke 22:31–34; Matthew 26:31–35; Mark 14:27–31_
- **In Gethsemane: Peter’s sword reveals his inner conflict**  
    _Matthew 26:36–46; Mark 14:32–42; Luke 22:45–51; John 18:10–11_
- **Peter follows at a distance: courage mixed with fear**  
    _Luke 22:54; Matthew 26:58_
- **The three denials: the breaking point**  
    _Luke 22:55–62; Matthew 26:69–75; Mark 14:66–72; John 18:15–27_

**3. CRUSHED: The Collapse of Self‑Confidence**

**Theme:** Peter’s greatest failure becomes the doorway to his transformation.

- **Peter follows at a distance: The conflict finally cracks**  
    _Luke 22:54; Matthew 26:58_
- **The courtyard fire: The place of collapse**  
    _Luke 22:55; John 18:18_
- **The three denials: The shattering of Peter’s identity**  
    _Luke 22:56–60; Matthew 26:69–74; Mark 14:66–71; John 18:17–27_
- **Jesus turns and looks at Peter: The moment that breaks him**  
    _Luke 22:61_
- **Peter weeps bitterly: The death of self‑reliance**  
    _Luke 22:62_

**4. COMMISSIONED: Restored and Rebuilt**

**Theme:** Jesus restores broken people and entrusts them with His mission.

- **Peter runs to the empty tomb: Hope awakens**  
    _Luke 24:9–12; John 20:3–8_
- **Jesus appears to Peter privately: Restoration begins**  
    _Luke 24:34; 1 Corinthians 15:5_
- **The Sea of Galilee: Jesus reenacts Peter’s calling**  
    _John 21:1–14_
- **“Do you love Me?”: Peter is restored and recommissioned**  
    _John 21:15–17_
- **Jesus prophesies Peter’s future: The conflicted man becomes the faithful martyr**  
    _John 21:18–19_
- **Peter leads the early church: The commission fulfilled**  
    _Acts 2–4_



Cracks in a Rock
Cracks in rocks are fractures or discontinuities that can form due to various geological processes such as stress, cooling, or tectonic activity. They can significantly influence the behavior of rocks under stress and play a crucial role in the development of faults and folds in geological formations...Understanding crack patterns helps geologists predict how rock formations will respond to applied stress.
